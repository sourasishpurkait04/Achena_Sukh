# Achena Sukh

Achena Sukh is the website for a registered nonprofit charitable trust serving communities across the Sundarban region and West Bengal, India.

Established on 22 March 2020, the organisation works to create lasting, positive change through education, health, relief, dignity, and community-development initiatives. Its guiding purpose is to bring meaningful happiness by helping people and easing hardship.

## What the website includes

- An introduction to Achena Sukh and its mission
- Information about current and upcoming community projects
- Stories and photographs from the organisation’s work
- Profiles of core members and volunteers
- Testimonials and community feedback
- Donation information and ways to support the cause

## Community impact

Achena Sukh supports underprivileged families and communities throughout West Bengal. The organisation reports serving more than 100,000 people, reaching over 75 villages, and contributing more than 4,000 hours of community work.

## Main areas of work

- Education support
- Health and relief initiatives
- Dignity and welfare programmes
- Community development

## Meaning and vision

The name *Achena Sukh* means “unknown happiness”: the deeper fulfilment found when people help one another and create meaningful change together. The organisation's vision is for compassionate, resilient communities where people have access to dignity, opportunity, and practical support.

The trust combines immediate assistance with long-term community care. Alongside responses to urgent needs, it supports efforts that can improve wellbeing, strengthen local participation, and give children and families more hopeful futures.

## Who we work with

Achena Sukh primarily serves communities in the Sundarban and other areas of West Bengal. Its outreach also extends beyond its home district, including indigenous communities in the Jangalmahal region and areas of Jhargram, as well as support for schools in Purulia.

The website is designed for community members, volunteers, donors, local partners, and anyone who wants to understand or support the organisation's work.

## How the main programmes help

### Education

Education programmes provide support for students and schools, helping young people continue their learning and build opportunities for the future.

### Health and relief

Health-related support, food distribution, and relief activities respond to immediate needs during difficult times.

### Food, clothing, and dignity

By providing meals, clothing, and essential assistance, the organisation helps individuals and families meet urgent needs with respect and care.

### Community development

Local programmes bring people together through practical support, volunteer action, and initiatives that improve everyday life.

### Environment

Tree plantation and environmental activities promote greener, more resilient communities and a healthier shared future.

### Inclusion and rights

Achena Sukh promotes inclusion, equal dignity, and compassion. Its work is rooted in the belief that every person deserves to be seen, respected, and supported.
